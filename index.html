    Learn About Your Menstrual Cycle

    Short, simple answers to common questions.

    Menstrual Phase

    This is when menstrual bleeding occurs. The uterus sheds its lining as a new cycle begins.

    Follicular Phase

    Follicles develop in the ovaries and the body prepares for ovulation. This phase overlaps with menstruation at its start.

    Ovulation

    An egg is released from the ovary. This usually happens around the middle of the cycle.

    Luteal Phase

    The body prepares for a possible pregnancy. If the egg is not fertilized, the cycle restarts with menstruation.

    What is a menstrual cycle?

    A menstrual cycle is the monthly process your body goes through to prepare for a possible pregnancy. It is counted from the first day of one period to the first day of the next.

    What is ovulation?

    Ovulation is the moment a mature egg is released from one of your ovaries. It typically happens about 14 days before your next period.

    What is a fertile window?

    The fertile window is the roughly six-day span when pregnancy is possible. Sperm can survive several days, so the window starts before ovulation and ends on ovulation day.

    Why can cycle lengths vary?

    Stress, sleep, illness, travel, hormonal changes and age can all shift your cycle length. A range of 21 to 45 days is common, so predictions are estimates, not guarantees.
